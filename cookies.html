Cookies and device storage

This page explains the small amount of browser technology currently used by SpaceSizer.

Cloudflare Web Analytics

SpaceSizer uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand page visits and website performance. Cloudflare describes this service as privacy-first and cookie-free. It does not use cookies or local storage to collect its usage metrics.

Saved SpaceSizer estimates

If you choose “Save result on this device”, the estimate is placed in your browser’s local storage. This is requested by you so the calculator can continue later. It is not an advertising cookie and is not automatically sent to SpaceSizer.

You can remove a saved estimate by clearing the site data for spacesizer.co.uk in your browser. A dedicated delete control may also be added to later versions.

External websites

Google Maps, email applications, WhatsApp and provider websites have their own cookie and privacy practices. Their policies apply after you choose to open those services.

Future changes

If SpaceSizer later introduces non-essential advertising, behavioural analytics or similar tracking, we will update this notice and request consent before activating it where required.
