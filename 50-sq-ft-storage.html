Storage size guide

50 sq ft storage: what could fit?

A 50 sq ft storage unit is a useful mid-size option for many small moves. Depending on item shape and packing, it can hold a mixture of furniture, appliances and boxes.

SpaceSizer can estimate whether your belongings are closer to 40, 50 or 60 sq ft and suggest a practical vehicle too.

Loads that may suit 50 sq ft

  • Contents from a studio or small one-bedroom property.
  • A sofa, bed, drawers, boxes and selected appliances.
  • Furniture during a house move or refurbishment.
  • Business stock, equipment or archived materials.

Vehicle guide

For SpaceSizer's current practical rules, loads up to around 50 sq ft are generally paired with a long-wheelbase cargo van guide. Exact vehicle capacity still depends heavily on item dimensions and loading style.

Should you go larger?

If you need frequent access, have several awkward items or do not want to stack heavily, a larger unit may be easier to use. SpaceSizer compares a tighter fit, a recommended option and a more comfortable option.
