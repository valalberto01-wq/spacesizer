Frequently asked questions

Plain-English answers about how SpaceSizer works and what its recommendations mean.

How does SpaceSizer calculate a storage size?

SpaceSizer estimates the volume of the belongings selected, then applies practical allowances for stacking and access. Full storage units use a configurable internal-height assumption; exact provider dimensions may differ.

Is the recommended size guaranteed?

No. It is a practical starting estimate. Item shape, dismantling, packaging, stacking, access requirements and the provider’s actual internal dimensions can change the final fit. Always confirm with the provider before booking.

Why are lockers treated differently?

A locker may be around 1 m × 1 m × 1 m and cannot safely be treated like a full-height 10 sq ft room. SpaceSizer uses actual locker dimensions and only suggests one for suitable box, bag or suitcase-type loads.

How accurate are household estimates?

They are editable starting lists based on typical belongings. Every household is different, so review the quantities and add or remove items before calculating.

How should I measure a custom item?

Measure the widest length, width and height, including packaging. Approximate centimetre measurements are acceptable, but allow extra space for unusually shaped or fragile items.

How is the vehicle guidance calculated?

SpaceSizer uses practical load bands: a long-wheelbase Transit for loads up to 50 sq ft, a Luton for loads above 50 and up to 100 sq ft, and a Maxi Low Loader for larger loads up to about 125 sq ft. The moving company must confirm the correct vehicle.

Are moving companies filtered by the recommended vehicle?

No. SpaceSizer shows broad local man-and-van, mover and removal-service searches regardless of the recommendation. The vehicle result is guidance only.

Where is my saved estimate kept?

It is stored locally in the browser on that device. It is not automatically uploaded to SpaceSizer. Clearing browser/site data will remove it.

Does SpaceSizer store my enquiry details?

The current enquiry tool prepares a message on your device and opens your own email or WhatsApp application. You review and send it yourself. SpaceSizer does not receive or retain that message through this tool.

Does SpaceSizer take payments or make bookings?

Not currently. Prices, availability, contracts, bookings and payments are agreed directly between you and the chosen provider.

Does SpaceSizer endorse the companies displayed?

No. External search results are independent. SpaceSizer does not currently rank, verify, endorse or guarantee the companies shown.
