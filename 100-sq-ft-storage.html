Storage size guide

100 sq ft storage: what could fit?

A 100 sq ft unit is a substantial storage space and can suit a larger household load or a sizeable amount of business stock, depending on the exact room shape and how items are packed.

Loads that may suit 100 sq ft

  • Contents from a larger flat or modest house move.
  • Multiple beds, sofas, wardrobes, appliances and boxes.
  • Furniture during renovations or relocation.
  • Business equipment, stock or archive storage.

Vehicle guide

SpaceSizer's current practical guide pairs loads above 50 sq ft and up to around 100 sq ft with a Luton-style vehicle. Real capacity varies by body size, wheel arches, loading method and whether furniture is dismantled.

Access matters

A unit that technically holds the load may still feel cramped if you need to reach items regularly. Allowing extra floor area for aisles and less aggressive stacking can make the unit much easier to use.
