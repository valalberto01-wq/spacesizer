Searching for a man and van near you?

The easiest way to get useful quotes is to know roughly how much you are moving before you contact providers.

Estimate the load first

SpaceSizer gives you a practical storage-size estimate and vehicle guide, then helps you search nearby moving providers using your location.

Size my move

What to include when asking for a quote

  • Pickup and delivery locations.
  • Number of floors, stairs or lift access.
  • Large furniture and appliance items.
  • Approximate number of boxes.
  • Parking restrictions or long carrying distances.
  • Whether you need one person or additional loading help.

Check the actual van

Vehicle names can be inconsistent. Ask the provider to confirm the internal cargo size if your load includes long or bulky items.

Local search through SpaceSizer
