Luton van capacity: when is it the right choice?

A Luton van is commonly used for household moves because its box-shaped body gives a more regular loading space than many panel vans.

When SpaceSizer suggests a Luton

As a practical guide, SpaceSizer currently pairs loads above 50 sq ft and up to around 100 sq ft of storage with a Luton-style vehicle.

  • Small to medium household moves.
  • Mixed loads of furniture, appliances and boxes.
  • Moves where a regular box-shaped cargo area makes stacking easier.

Approximate volume

Many Luton vans offer roughly 18.5–20 m³ of cargo space, but actual dimensions vary by manufacturer and conversion.

What can reduce usable capacity?

Fragile furniture, long items, poor stacking, load securing requirements and awkward shapes can all reduce how much fits in practice. A lift or tail-lift can also affect the exact body configuration.
