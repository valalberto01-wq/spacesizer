Searching for self storage near you?

Before comparing prices, work out approximately how much space you need. Booking too small can create access problems, while booking much larger than necessary can increase cost.

What to compare when choosing storage

  • Unit size and exact internal dimensions.
  • Access hours and whether you need regular access.
  • Ground-floor versus upper-floor access.
  • Insurance requirements and security arrangements.
  • Minimum stay, notice periods and promotional pricing.
  • Collection, delivery or van-hire options.

A quoted floor area does not tell you everything. Room shape, ceiling height, pillars and door width can all affect what fits.

Why SpaceSizer helps

Instead of starting with a provider's size chart, you can start with your actual belongings. Once you have a practical size estimate, comparing local storage becomes much easier.
