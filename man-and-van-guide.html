Moving guide

How to choose a man and van for your move.

A man-and-van service can be a flexible option for small and medium moves, but the right provider needs enough vehicle capacity, time and handling help for your actual belongings.

Estimate your load first

SpaceSizer can give you a practical storage size and vehicle guide before you contact a moving provider.

Size my move

What to tell the provider

  • Your collection and delivery locations.
  • Whether there are stairs, lifts or restricted parking.
  • The main furniture and appliance items.
  • An approximate number and size of boxes.
  • Any very heavy, fragile, long or awkward items.
  • Whether furniture will be dismantled before collection.

Ask about the vehicle

Do not rely on a generic label alone. Two vans described as “LWB” or “Luton” can have different internal dimensions and payloads.

When a man and van may suit you

It can work well for storage collections, small home moves, student moves, furniture transport and loads that do not require a large removals crew. Larger or more complex moves may need additional people, multiple loads or a full removals service.

SpaceSizer does not guarantee third-party transport providers. Confirm availability, insurance, pricing and vehicle suitability directly.
