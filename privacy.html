Privacy notice

This notice explains what happens to information when you use SpaceSizer. We have written it in plain language and will update it as the service develops.

Who is responsible?

SpaceSizer is a UK-based digital project operating the website at spacesizer.co.uk. For privacy questions or requests, email info@spacesizer.co.uk.

Information used by the calculator

The calculator uses the belongings, quantities, measurements and access preferences you enter to produce an estimate. In the current website version this calculation happens in your browser.

Saved results

If you choose to save a result, it is stored locally in that browser on that device. SpaceSizer does not automatically receive the saved inventory. Clearing the site data in your browser will remove it.

Provider enquiries

The current enquiry feature prepares a branded message on your device. If you choose email, your email application opens; if you choose WhatsApp, WhatsApp opens. You review and send the message yourself to the company details you entered.

SpaceSizer does not receive or retain the enquiry through that tool. The chosen company and the email or messaging service will process the information according to their own privacy notices. Do not send sensitive information that the company does not need.

Emails sent to SpaceSizer

If you contact info@spacesizer.co.uk, we receive the information in your message and your contact details. We use this to answer you, manage the service and keep appropriate business records. Our lawful bases are normally taking steps at your request and our legitimate interests in operating and improving SpaceSizer.

Website analytics and security

We use Cloudflare to deliver, protect and measure the website. Cloudflare Web Analytics is described by Cloudflare as cookie-free and as not collecting or using visitors’ personal data. Hosting and security systems may still process technical request information as needed to deliver and protect the site.

External services

SpaceSizer may open Google Maps, email, WhatsApp or third-party provider websites when you choose those options. SpaceSizer does not control those services. Their privacy terms apply once opened.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only where you direct it, where a service supplier must process it to operate or secure SpaceSizer, or where disclosure is required by law.

How long information is kept

  • Saved estimates remain on your device until you clear them or clear browser data.
  • Messages sent to SpaceSizer are kept only as long as reasonably needed to answer, manage records, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations.
  • Technical security records are retained according to the relevant service provider’s settings and policies.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights to access, correct or delete personal information, restrict or object to processing, and obtain a copy of certain information. You may also withdraw consent where consent is relied upon.

To exercise a right, email info@spacesizer.co.uk. You may complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

SpaceSizer is intended for people arranging storage or moving services and is not designed to collect information from children.

Changes

We will update this page when the app introduces accounts, registered providers, direct server-based enquiries, payments or new analytics.
