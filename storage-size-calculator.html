How storage sizing works

Storage units are normally sold by floor area. The amount you can actually fit depends on the height of the unit, the shape of your belongings, whether furniture can be dismantled, how safely items can be stacked and whether you want an aisle for access.

Tight fit

Best when you are happy to stack carefully and do not expect to access belongings often.

Recommended fit

A balanced starting point that allows some practical access while still using the space efficiently.

More comfortable

Useful when you want easier access, less stacking or extra flexibility for awkward items.

Common questions

Should I book the smallest size that technically fits?

Not always. A slightly larger unit may make loading, access and future retrieval much easier.

Do boxes make a big difference?

Yes. A large number of boxes can use substantial volume, but they are usually easier to stack efficiently than sofas, bikes or wardrobes.

Can SpaceSizer guarantee a fit?

No. It is an estimate because exact item and unit dimensions vary.

Estimate only. Confirm exact unit dimensions and access with the storage provider before booking.
